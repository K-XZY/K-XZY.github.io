Decent-VLA: An Empirical Study and Open Testbed for Federated Fine-Tuning of Vision-Language-Action Models submitted to ICRA 2027
Zhekai Duan*, Kevin Ziyang Xie, Xinyu Tan, Shikai Geng, Chengxu Zhou, Ramana Kompella, Gaowen Liu, Chris Xiaoxuan Lu
federated-learning VLA robot-learning

We study federated fine-tuning of three pretrained VLA policies across the 40 simulated tasks of LIBERO and two real-robot deployments of two and three sites, covering the gap to centralized fine-tuning, the choice of federated parameter scope, the aggregation algorithm, robustness under evaluation-time distribution shift, and the cross-site heterogeneity that appears only on physical robots; we release Decent-VLA, the testbed behind the study.

Research Projects
CRL-Gen: Can unsupervised RL act as a universal demonstrator?
Kevin Ziyang Xie, Zhekai Duan
June 2026
contrastive-rl reinforcement-learning multi-arm-manipulation

Contrastive RL as a universal demonstrator: goal-conditioned manipulation learned without human demonstration priors, then distilled into VLA models. The same recipe holds across one, two, and three arms. It stopped short of publication because it introduced no new method — and existing CRL cannot explore a space this combinatorial, so long-horizon and multi-object tasks stay out of reach. However, such exploration-driven learning deserves further investigation, as it enables a robot to learn novel manipulation behaviours that use its full body, and does not rely on human data.
